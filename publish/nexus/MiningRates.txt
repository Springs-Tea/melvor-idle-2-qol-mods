DESCRIPTION (paste as is: it is in Nexus formatting)

[size=5][b]Mining Rates[/b][/size]
Every rock has its own time per hit, its own XP and its own ore, and some give gems or other items on the side. This window puts them side by side, with the time for one ore and the ore, Mining XP and gold an hour at each rock gives your character.

[size=4][b]Where it is[/b][/size]
On the Mining page, a round button with the Mining icon joins the others on the right edge of the screen. Click it to open the window, and again, or the X, to close it. On any other page the window steps aside, and it is back as you left it when you return to Mining.

[size=4][b]What the window shows[/b][/size]
One line per rock the Mining page shows you, starting with the rock's picture and its category, and:
[list]
[*][b]Time[/b]: how long one ore takes, with your bonuses and the hits a break takes counted in.
[*][b]Ore[/b]: the rock's ore per hour.
[*][b]XP[/b]: Mining XP per hour.
[*][b]Gold[/b]: what the ore and the other items of an hour sell for.
[/list]
The best number of each column is in gold. Click a column's name to sort by it; the first click puts the best rocks on top. The rock you are mining has a lit outline. A rock you cannot mine yet is greyed, with the level it needs. A rock you hid on the Mining page is listed only while the page lists hidden rocks too.
Click a line to see that rock on the Mining page: the page's category filter goes to the rock's category when it is on another one, and its list of rocks scrolls until the rock's whole card is in sight beside the window. When the page has no room for that, the line's tooltip says so: drag the window aside. Nothing is started or stopped, and the window stays open.

[size=4][b]In the tooltip of a line[/b][/size]
The whole hour at that rock: the time of a hit, how many hits a break takes and the breaks of an hour, the XP per hour and per break, the break chance and the ore of a break and of an hour, every item of a hit with its chance per hit and how many an hour brings, and the gold.

[size=4][b]What is counted[/b][/size]
The numbers start from the game's own values for each rock, including its time per hit, so they change with your bonuses. The rock's card says, "Your chance to break the rock per hit. Rock automatically breaks when a sum of 100% is reached." A hit that misses adds its chance to the sum. The window reads the minimum break bar as the starting bar after a break. With a chance of 100% or higher, every hit breaks the rock. The tooltip shows the same chance as the card. On Runite Ore, breaks took 4.71 hits on average. Its chance and minimum gave 4.53 by the rule.

Items from hits use the chances under "On Hit Rewards". The window reads the game's "Your chance to double the output" as applying to both the break product and the items of a hit. It averages those chances over many hits and uses the game's sell prices for gold. A dash marks a rate the window cannot count. Hover for the reason.

Runite Ore gave Mining XP on 85 of 85 breaks and 0 of 318 other hits. Topaz Vein gave XP and Uncut Topaz on 92 of 92 breaks and 0 of 708 other hits. The bank gained one gem on 82 plain breaks and two on 10, averaging 1.109 per break against the window's estimate of 1.07.
The window counts XP once per break on every other rock too. Time per ore, ore per hour and the gold from that ore all count the listed product on a break, as measured on Topaz Vein.

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]It is an estimate, not a promise: chances average out over many hits.
[*]A chance to shatter a rock and a chance of more ore are not counted; the tooltip says so when you have one, by the name the Mining page gives it. A rock's own stone, essence or bar is not counted either.
[*]Mining has no mastery XP in this game, so there is no column for it.
[*]The window starts nothing. A click on a line only changes the page's category filter and where its list of rocks is scrolled; mining, the rocks you hid and whether the page lists them are left as they are.
[*]A rock the game has no picture for yet shows none, rather than the game's grey stand-in.
[*]The window first opens beside the rock cards where there is room. Drag it to move it, resize it from its bottom right corner, or fold it down to its title. Its place, size, fold and sort order are kept in its own folder.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/melvoridle2/mods/2]Melvor Mod Loader[/url] (0.1.3 or newer).
[*]Extract this zip into the game folder: Steam > right-click Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files. The file ends up as [font=Courier New]mods\MiningRates\MiningRates.dll[/font].
[*]Start the game.

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete the folder [font=Courier New]mods\MiningRates[/font]. Nothing in your save is changed.

[size=4][b]About[/b][/size]
Made for game version 0.11.6-alpha.1 with Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3. The game is in early access: an update can break the mod until it is rebuilt. It then switches itself off and says so in MelvorModLoader.log; the game and your other mods keep running.

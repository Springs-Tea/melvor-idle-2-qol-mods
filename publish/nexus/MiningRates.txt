Where do I mine for the most XP? On the Mining page, a round button opens a window with one line per rock: Mining XP per hour, with the time of an ore, ore and gold per hour to come, the best of each column in gold, sortable, and in each line's tooltip the whole hour: the ore of a break and every item of a hit with its chance.

DESCRIPTION (paste as is: it is in Nexus formatting)

[size=5][b]Mining Rates[/b][/size]
Every rock has its own time per hit, its own XP and its own ore, and some give gems or other items on the side. To compare them you would open the rocks one by one and work out the hour in your head. This window puts them side by side, with what an hour at each rock gives your character.

[size=4][b]Where it is[/b][/size]
On the Mining page, a round button with the Mining icon joins the others on the right edge of the screen. Click it to open the window, and again, or the X, to close it. On any other page the window steps aside, and it is back as you left it when you return to Mining.

[size=4][b]What the window shows[/b][/size]
One line per rock the Mining page shows you, starting with the rock's picture and its category, and per hour:
[list]
[*][b]Time[/b]: how long one ore takes.
[*][b]Ore[/b]: the rock's ore, which comes with a break.
[*][b]XP[/b]: Mining XP, the XP of the rock's XP tag on every hit.
[*][b]Gold[/b]: what the ore and the items sell for.
[/list]
How many hits it takes to break a rock is not a number the game shows, and the window does not make one up: for now the time of an ore, the ore and the gold show -.
The best number of each column is in gold. Click a column's name to sort by it; the first click puts the best rocks on top. The rock you are mining has a lit outline. A rock you cannot mine yet is greyed, with the level it needs. A rock you hid on the Mining page is listed only while the page's Show hidden box is ticked.
Click a line to see that rock on the Mining page: the page's category filter goes to the rock's category when it is on another one, and its list of rocks scrolls to the rock's card. Nothing is started or stopped, and the window stays open.

[size=4][b]In the tooltip of a line[/b][/size]
The whole hour at that rock: the time of a hit, the break chance, the XP per hour and per hit, the ore of a break, every item of a hit with its chance per hit and how many an hour brings, and the gold.

[size=4][b]What is counted[/b][/size]
The numbers start from the game's own values for each rock, the ones its card on the Mining page shows, so whatever changes those changes the window too. A hit takes the rock's interval, every hit gives the XP of the rock's XP tag (the game's XP per action), the items of a hit come at their chance on every hit, the doubling chance doubles the ore and the items of a hit, and chances count as their long-run average.

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]It is an estimate, not a promise: chances average out over many hits.
[*]How many hits a break takes is kept by the game to itself; until it is known, the time of an ore, the ore and the gold show -.
[*]A chance to shatter a rock and a chance of more ore are not counted; the tooltip says so when you have one, by the name the Mining page gives it.
[*]Mining has no mastery XP in this game, so there is no column for it.
[*]The window starts nothing. A click on a line only changes the page's category filter and where its list of rocks is scrolled; mining, the rocks you hid and the Show hidden box are left as they are.
[*]A rock the game has no picture for yet shows none, rather than the game's grey stand-in.
[*]Drag the window to move it, resize it from its bottom right corner, or fold it down to its title. Its place, size, fold and sort order are kept in its own folder.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/melvoridle2/mods/2]Melvor Mod Loader[/url] (0.1.3 or newer).
[*]Extract this zip into the game folder: Steam > right-click Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files. The file ends up as [font=Courier New]mods\MiningRates\MiningRates.dll[/font].
[*]Start the game.
[/list]
To update, extract the new zip over the old one.

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete the folder [font=Courier New]mods\MiningRates[/font]. Nothing in your save is changed.

Made for game version 0.11.6-alpha.1 with Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3. The game is in early access: an update can break the mod until it is rebuilt. It then switches itself off and says so in MelvorModLoader.log; the game and your other mods keep running.

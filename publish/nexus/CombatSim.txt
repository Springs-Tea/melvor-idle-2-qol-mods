DESCRIPTION (paste as is: it is in Nexus formatting)

[size=5][b]Combat Simulator[/b][/size]
What does an hour of this fight give me, what does it cost, and will I die? This answers it without fighting.

It does [b]not[/b] re-create the game's combat formulas. It loads a copy of your character into a second, invisible game and runs the game's own combat on it, in the background. An hour of fighting takes a fraction of a second. Nothing the copy does is saved.

[size=4][b]What it does[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Simulate[/b] an area (every enemy in it), a dungeon, or everything you have unlocked, for 15 minutes to 12 hours of game time each.
[*][b]Results per hour[/b]: kills, combat XP, gold, food eaten, deaths, and the lowest your hitpoints got. Click a column to sort.
[*][b]Details[/b]: XP per skill, loot item by item, what was used (food, ammo, runes, prayer points), damage taken, time of the first death.
[*][b]Equipment[/b]: fight with one of your saved loadouts, or click any slot to try another item: every item of the game that fits, with a search box, whether you own it or not. The same fight with other equipment is compared side by side.
[*][b]Best for[/b]: pick the skill you want to train. Every unlocked fight is tried in each attack stance of your weapon and the fights are ranked by what they give that skill.
[*][b]Fight[/b]: a button starts the selected fight in the game.
[*][b]Supplies[/b]: unlimited (the rates of a fight that is kept supplied) or from your bank (how far your food, ammo and runes get you).
[/list]
A round button on the right edge of the screen opens the window. It can be dragged and resized.

[size=4][b]Limits[/b][/size]
Fights are random, so two runs of the same fight differ a little; longer fights are steadier. An item whose requirements you do not meet cannot be equipped in the copy either. Farming and Ranching are paused in the copy.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/melvoridle2/mods/2]Melvor Mod Loader[/url] (0.1.3 or newer).
[*]Extract this zip into the game folder: Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files. The file ends up as [font=Courier New]mods\CombatSim\CombatSim.dll[/font].
[*]Start the game.
[/list]
[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete the folder [font=Courier New]mods\CombatSim[/font]. Nothing in your save is changed.

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
Tested on game version 0.11.6-alpha.1 with Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3. The game is in early access: an update can break the mod until it is rebuilt.
It uses the game's own textures, fonts and colours, so it looks like part of the game.

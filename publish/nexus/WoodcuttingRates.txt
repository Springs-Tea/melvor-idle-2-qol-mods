SUMMARY (the short line under the name, at most 350 characters)
Which tree gives the most XP? On the Woodcutting page, a round button opens a window with one line per tree: the time of a log, and logs, Woodcutting XP and gold per hour, the best in gold, sortable. Each line's tooltip has the hits a cut takes and every item a hit or a cut can bring with its chance. A click shows the tree's card on the page.

DESCRIPTION (paste as is: it is in Nexus formatting)

[size=5][b]Woodcutting Rates[/b][/size]
Every tree has its own time per hit, its own hit points, its own XP and its own logs, and some give other items on the side. A tree takes several hits to cut, so the time a card shows is not the time of a log. To compare the trees you would open them one by one and work out the hour in your head. This window puts them side by side, with what an hour at each tree gives your character.

[size=4][b]Where it is[/b][/size]
On the Woodcutting page, a round button with the Woodcutting icon joins the others on the right edge of the screen. Click it to open the window, and again, or the X, to close it. On any other page the window steps aside, and it is back as you left it when you return to Woodcutting.

[size=4][b]What the window shows[/b][/size]
One line per tree the Woodcutting page shows you, starting with the tree's picture and its category, and:
[list]
[*][b]Time[/b]: how long one log takes.
[*][b]Logs[/b]: the tree's logs per hour.
[*][b]XP[/b]: Woodcutting XP per hour.
[*][b]Gold[/b]: what the logs and the other items of an hour sell for.
[/list]
The hour rests on the cuts of an hour: the logs come with a cut, as a tree's card lists them under "Full Cut", and so does the XP. The window works out the hits a cut takes from the tree's hit points and your chances.
The best number of each column is in gold. Click a column's name to sort by it; the first click puts the best trees on top. The tree you are cutting has a lit outline. A tree you cannot cut yet is greyed, with the level it needs. A tree you hid on the Woodcutting page is listed only while the page lists hidden trees too.
Click a line to see that tree on the Woodcutting page: the page's category filter goes to the tree's category when it is on another one, and its list of trees scrolls until the tree's whole card is in sight beside the window. When the page has no room for that, the line's tooltip says so: drag the window aside. Nothing is started or stopped, and the window stays open.

[size=4][b]In the tooltip of a line[/b][/size]
The hour at that tree: the time of a hit, the XP per hour and per cut, the tree's hit points, the hits a cut takes and the cuts of an hour, your chances of double damage, a weak spot and an instant cut, the logs, every item a hit or a cut can bring with its chance, and the overgrowth on the tree now.

[size=4][b]What is counted[/b][/size]
The numbers start from the game's own values for each tree, so whatever changes those changes the window too. A hit takes the tree's time of a hit, and chances count as their long-run average. Measured in the game, on 597 hits of a Maple Tree: a hit takes 1 hit point from the tree and a double hit 2 (6.76 hits a cut were measured, and this rule gives 6.755), and the XP comes with a cut only: on 88 of 88 cuts and on none of 509 other hits, the XP tag's "The Skill XP gained per action." with a cut as the action. A weak spot and an instant cut did not come there, so they follow the game's own words, read as they say: a weak spot is "dealing 3 damage + 10% of the Tree's Max HP", not rounded, instead of the hit's 1, and an instant cut is "to instantly reduce a Tree's HP to 0". The logs come with a cut and the other items with a hit or a cut, in the game's own words: a tree's card lists them under "Per Hit" and "Full Cut". These are the window's reading: the doubling chance doubles the logs and the items, from its words, "Your chance to double the output"; the overgrowth on a tree now is in its numbers.

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]It is an estimate, not a promise: chances average out over many hits.
[*]How much a hit takes and when the XP comes were measured on one character's cutting. A weak spot and an instant cut did not come there, so for them the window follows the game's words.
[*]Woodcutting has no mastery XP in this game, so there is no column for it.
[*]The overgrowth on a tree now is in its numbers; what a cut may put on later is not counted.
[*]The window starts nothing. A click on a line only changes the page's category filter and where its list of trees is scrolled; woodcutting, the trees you hid and whether the page lists them are left as they are.
[*]A tree the game has no picture for yet shows none, rather than the game's grey stand-in.
[*]Until you move it, the window opens at the right side of the page, beside the trees' cards. Drag the window to move it, resize it from its bottom right corner, or fold it down to its title. Its place, size, fold and sort order are kept in its own folder.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/melvoridle2/mods/2]Melvor Mod Loader[/url] (0.1.3 or newer).

[size=4][b]About[/b][/size]
Made for game version 0.11.6-alpha.1 with Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3. The game is in early access: an update can break the mod until it is rebuilt. It then switches itself off and says so in MelvorModLoader.log; the game and your other mods keep running.
It uses the game's own textures, fonts and colours, so it looks like part of the game. It gives no items, XP or bonuses.

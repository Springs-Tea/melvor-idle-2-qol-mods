DESCRIPTION (paste as is: it is in Nexus formatting)

[size=5][b]Quality of Life Mods[/b][/size]
Thirteen mods that show what the game already knows but does not tell you. Each one is also a page of its own, if you only want some of them: this zip is all thirteen.

[list]
[*][b]Combat Simulator[/b]: what an hour of each fight gives, uses and risks, worked out by running the game's own combat on a hidden copy of your character. Compare loadouts, items and spells, find the best place to train a skill, look at earlier scans again.
[*][b]Item Sources[/b]: hover "Found in" or "Used in" on an item to see which enemy drops it and where, how many and how likely, and every recipe, chest or shop purchase that gives or uses it. Alt keeps any item's tooltip open, anywhere in the game.
[*][b]Item Encyclopedia[/b]: every item of the game in one window, with a search and filters by kind, slot, combat style, weapon, skill and source, and everything about each item: stats, requirements, where it drops and how likely, what it is made from and what it is used in.
[*][b]Recipe Rates[/b]: XP per hour on every recipe of a skill's list, and the whole hour in the XP tooltip.
[*][b]Fishing Rates[/b]: every fishing spot side by side, with its fish: time per catch, and catches, Fishing XP, gold and bait per hour, the best of each column in gold, and the whole hour in each line's tooltip. A click on a line takes the Fishing page to that spot.
[*][b]Mining Rates[/b]: every rock side by side: time per ore, and ore, Mining XP and gold per hour by the game's own rule for breaking a rock, the best of each column in gold, and the whole hour in each line's tooltip. A click on a line shows that rock's card on the Mining page.
[*][b]Thieving Overview[/b]: every Thieving target of every place you have found in one list: success, time per try, and XP, gold, rare items and food per hour, Safe or Risky, the best of each column in gold, and the whole hour in each line's tooltip. Best for an item finds the targets that give it, and View Possible Rewards gets the number per hour of each reward.
[*][b]Skill Bonuses[/b]: where a skill's bonuses come from. A round button on a skill page opens a window with every stat of the page's stats panel and, under it, every item, pet, shop purchase, constellation and other source the game counts, with what each one gives.
[*][b]Astrology Overview[/b]: your constellations in one window, those with a node choice ready first, then the shortest study time to the next unlock, with the knowledge it needs, the bonus every minor node gives, and what a locked one still misses.
[*][b]Away Summary[/b]: switch to another app or minimise the game while your character trains, and come back to how long you were away and what changed: XP and levels, items gained and used, gold, and when an action stopped.
[*][b]Combat Goals[/b]: XP per hour and the time left to a level goal, for every combat skill you are training.
[*][b]Kills in Combat[/b]: how many times you have killed the enemy you are fighting, next to its name.
[*][b]Dungeon Completions[/b]: how many times you have completed each dungeon, in the combat areas menu.
[/list]
They use the game's own textures, fonts and colours, so they look like part of the game. They give no items, XP or bonuses, and nothing in your save is changed.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/melvoridle2/mods/2]Melvor Mod Loader[/url] (0.1.3 or newer).
[*]Extract this zip into the game folder: Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files. Every mod ends up in a folder of its own under [font=Courier New]mods[/font].
[*]Start the game.
[/list]
[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete the folders of the mods you do not want from [font=Courier New]mods[/font]. Do not install this zip and the single mods' zips in different versions: the newer file wins, which is fine, but keep them the same.

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
Tested on game version 0.11.6-alpha.1 with Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3. The game is in early access: an update can break a mod until it is rebuilt; it then switches itself off and says so in MelvorModLoader.log.

On the Fishing page, a round button opens a window with one line per fishing spot and its fish: time per catch, catches, Fishing XP, gold and bait per hour, the best of each column in gold, sortable, and in each line's tooltip the whole hour: every fish and item with its chance.

DESCRIPTION (paste as is: it is in Nexus formatting)

[size=5][b]Fishing Rates[/b][/size]
Every fishing spot has its own catch time, its own XP and its own fish, some with a chance at something rarer. To compare them you would open the spots one by one and work out the hour in your head. This window puts them side by side, with what an hour at each spot gives your character.

[size=4][b]Where it is[/b][/size]
On the Fishing page, a round button with the Fishing icon joins the others on the right edge of the screen. Click it to open the window, and again, or the X, to close it. On any other page the window steps aside, and it is back as you left it when you return to Fishing.

[size=4][b]What the window shows[/b][/size]
One line per fishing spot the Fishing page shows you, starting with the pictures of its fish, and per hour:
[list]
[*][b]Time[/b]: how long one catch takes, the middle of the spot's catch time.
[*][b]Catches[/b]: fish and junk together.
[*][b]XP[/b]: Fishing XP.
[*][b]Gold[/b]: what the catches sell for.
[*][b]Bait[/b]: the bait selected on the Fishing page, used per hour, while one is selected.
[/list]
The best number of each column is in gold. Click a column's name to sort by it; the first click puts the best spots on top. The spot the Fishing page shows has a lit outline. A spot you cannot fish yet is greyed, with the level it needs.
Click a line to go to that spot: the Fishing page opens its location and selects the spot, as your own two clicks there would. A locked spot opens its location only. Nothing is started or stopped, and the window stays open.

[size=4][b]In the tooltip of a line[/b][/size]
The whole hour at that spot: the catch time, the XP per hour and per catch, every fish, junk and special item with its chance per catch and how many an hour brings, the gold and how much of it is special items, and the bait with how long what is in your bank lasts.

[size=4][b]What is counted[/b][/size]
The numbers start from the game's own values for each spot, the ones its list of spots shows, so whatever changes those changes the window too. A catch takes the middle of the game's catch time, every catch gives the spot's XP (with its fraction: the Fishing page's XP tag shows a whole number), special items come on top of a catch, and chances count as their long-run average.

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]It is an estimate, not a promise: chances average out over many catches.
[*]With a priority fish set at a spot, which fish come is the game's own choice: that spot's fish and gold are left out rather than guessed.
[*]Bonuses that turn junk into gold, reroll junk or bring a second special item are not counted; the tooltip says so when you have one, by the name the Fishing page gives it.
[*]Fishing has no mastery XP in this game, so there is no column for it.
[*]The window starts nothing. A click on a line only changes which location and spot the Fishing page shows; fishing, the bait and the priority fish are left as they are.
[*]A fish the game has no picture for yet shows none, rather than the game's grey stand-in. The tooltip still names every fish.
[*]Drag the window to move it, resize it from its bottom right corner, or fold it down to its title. Its place, size, fold and sort order are kept in its own folder.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/melvoridle2/mods/2]Melvor Mod Loader[/url] (0.1.3 or newer).
[*]Extract this zip into the game folder: Steam > right-click Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files. The file ends up as [font=Courier New]mods\FishingRates\FishingRates.dll[/font].
[*]Start the game.
[/list]
To update, extract the new zip over the old one.

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete the folder [font=Courier New]mods\FishingRates[/font]. Nothing in your save is changed.

[size=4][b]About[/b][/size]
Made for game version 0.11.6-alpha.1 with Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3. The game is in early access: an update can break the mod until it is rebuilt. It then switches itself off and says so in MelvorModLoader.log; the game and your other mods keep running.
It uses the game's own textures, fonts and colours, so it looks like part of the game. It gives no items, XP or bonuses.

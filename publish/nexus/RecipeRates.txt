[size=5][b]Recipe Rates[/b][/size]
Which recipe gives more XP per hour: the one that takes 60 seconds for 10 XP, or the one that takes 20 seconds for 3? This shows it before you make anything.

[list]
[*]In a skill's recipe list, every recipe shows its [b]XP per hour[/b] under its XP tag. The best one in the list is gold.
[*]Hover the XP tag for [b]the whole hour[/b]: XP, the items made, the items used, what the products sell for, how long one takes, and how long the materials in your bank will last.
[/list]
Works for Smithing, Crafting, Fletching, Runecrafting, Beastcrafting, Cooking, Firemaking and Herblore.

The numbers come from the game's own values, so every bonus you have (equipment, potions, mastery) is in them. Chances count as their long-run average: doubling, preservation, skipped steps, and failed attempts in Cooking. It is an estimate, not a promise.

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/melvoridle2/mods/2]Melvor Mod Loader[/url] (0.1.3 or newer).
[*]Extract this zip into the game folder: Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files. The file ends up as [font=Courier New]mods\RecipeRates\RecipeRates.dll[/font].
[*]Start the game.
[/list]
[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete the folder [font=Courier New]mods\RecipeRates[/font]. Nothing in your save is changed.

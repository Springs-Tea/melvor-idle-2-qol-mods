SUMMARY (the short line under the name, at most 350 characters)
Where do I hunt? On the Hunting page, a round button opens a window with one line per animal: its location, the time of a catch, and the catches, Hunting XP, gold and traps of an hour, from the game's own numbers. Each line's tooltip has the whole hour, step by step. A click opens the animal.

DESCRIPTION (paste as is: it is in Nexus formatting)

[size=5][b]Hunting Rates[/b][/size]
Every animal has its own hunt: surveying until its tracks are found, a number of tracking steps, and a trap that may fail and let the animal escape. Each step has its own time and chance, and the animal has its own output, with a chance at a rare species. To compare them you would open the animals one by one and add up the steps in your head. This window puts them side by side, with the game's own numbers for your character.

[size=4][b]Where it is[/b][/size]
On the Hunting page, a round button with the Hunting icon joins the others on the right edge of the screen. Click it to open the window, and again, or the X, to close it. On any other page the window steps aside, and it is back as you left it when you return to Hunting.

[size=4][b]What the window shows[/b][/size]
One line per animal the Hunting page can show you, starting with the animal's picture and its location, and:
[list]
[*][b]Time[/b]: how long one catch takes, surveying, tracking and trapping, escapes included.
[*][b]Catches[/b]: animals caught per hour.
[*][b]XP[/b]: Hunting XP per hour.
[*][b]Gold[/b]: what the output of an hour sells for, rare extras too but no collectable.
[*][b]Traps[/b]: traps taken from the bank per hour, one every try, preserved or not.
[/list]
How the steps of a hunt join into a catch is in no number of the game's: it was measured on a character's own hunting. An escape sends you back to tracking with more steps to go, and how many is known only where every reading of it gives the same number; elsewhere a column shows "-" rather than a guess, and a note under the list says why. For now that is most animals. A line is also "-" until the Hunting page has written how many surveys a hunt of that animal takes: select the animal there once. The window reads what the page writes whenever the page is on the screen, window open or not, and keeps it for each of your characters in its own file, so a line keeps its numbers from one game to the next; the tooltip says "as the Hunting page last wrote it" for a number kept from an earlier game.
The best number of each column is in gold. Click a column's name to sort by it; the first click puts the best animals on top. The animal the Hunting page shows has a lit outline. An animal you cannot hunt yet is greyed, with the level it needs. An animal you have not discovered yet gives nothing away: only its name as the page writes it, its location and what it needs. Each picture is drawn as the animal's card on the Hunting page last drew it, a black shape where the card draws one; until the page has drawn its picture, a black shape for an animal the journal counts no catch of, in colour once it counts one. With the game's spider setting on, no spider is shown.
Click a line to open that animal on the Hunting page. While you are not hunting, its location and the animal are selected through the page's own buttons, as your own clicks there would. While you hunt, a click selects nothing, since that might change the hunt: it only shows the hunt instead of the journal, and the animal's card when the page shows its location. Nothing is started or stopped, and the window stays open.

[size=4][b]In the tooltip of a line[/b][/size]
The whole hour of that animal: the surveys of a hunt, the steps of tracking and the time of one, the time and chance of a try of the trap, the chance to try again, what an escape costs, the time of a catch, the Hunting XP and the journal XP, the output with the chance of each item, the rare species in one line, the trap and the bait with how long the bank's last, the gold, and what is not counted: the chance to skip a step, the preservation chance, what surveying finds, and what the animal's level adds to the average. A rare species you have never trapped and a collectable you have never found are not named, as the journal does.

[size=4][b]What is counted[/b][/size]
The numbers start from the game's own values for each animal, so whatever changes those changes the window too. The ranges of the XP and of the surveys of a hunt are the ones the Hunting page writes for each animal, read from the page. Measured on 15 minutes of hunting: every tracking step takes one interval and never fails; a failed try of the trap is tried again at the game's reattempt chance, otherwise the animal escapes back to tracking with more steps to go; every try takes one trap and one bait from the bank, preserved or not; the XP and the output come with a catch. Not counted: the chance to skip a step, the preservation chance, what surveying finds, the extras of a rare species still locked, and what an animal's level adds to the average, which the page says it does (the middle of XP and output was measured on 6 catches of Yellowtail Bird, at an animal level from 1 to 7). The window's own reading: the middle of each of the game's ranges, chances as their long-run average, and the doubling chance on the output only.

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]It is an estimate, not a promise: chances average out over many hunts. An escape costs several steps, so one hunt can take twice as long as the next.
[*]Hunting has no mastery XP in this game, so there is no column for it. An animal's journal XP is in the tooltip.
[*]Gold is not profit: traps and bait are not taken off.
[*]The window starts nothing. While you are not hunting, a click on a line changes which location and animal the Hunting page selects, through the page's own buttons; while you hunt, it selects nothing. Hunting, traps, bait and hunting without a trap are left as they are.
[*]An animal the game has no picture for yet shows none, rather than the game's grey stand-in.
[*]Until you move it, the window opens at the corner of the Hunting page where the most of its animals and locations stay in sight beside it. Drag the window to move it, resize it from its bottom right corner, or fold it down to its title. Its place, size, fold and sort order are kept in its own folder.
[/list]

[size=4][b]About[/b][/size]
Made for game version 0.11.6-alpha.1 with Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3. The game is in early access: an update can break the mod until it is rebuilt. It then switches itself off and says so in MelvorModLoader.log; the game and your other mods keep running.
It uses the game's own textures, fonts and colours, so it looks like part of the game. It gives no items, XP or bonuses.

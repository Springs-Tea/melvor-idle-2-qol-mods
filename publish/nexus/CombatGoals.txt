SUMMARY (the short line under the name)
A small window with the XP per hour of every combat skill you are training and the time left to a level goal you set for each.

DESCRIPTION (paste as is: it is in Nexus formatting)

[size=5][b]Combat Goals[/b][/size]
The game's footer tells you how long a skill takes to reach a level goal, but only for one skill. Combat trains several at once.

[list]
[*]A small window lists every combat skill that is gaining XP, with its [b]XP per hour[/b] and the [b]time left[/b] to a level goal.
[*]Type a goal level per skill. Goals are remembered per character.
[*]The rate is the average of the current fight over the last 1, 5, 10 or 30 minutes. Leaving combat, or fighting something else, starts the measurement again.
[*]Drag the window anywhere; drag its corner for another size.
[*]A round button on the right edge of the screen, and F10, show and hide it.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/melvoridle2/mods/2]Melvor Mod Loader[/url] (0.1.3 or newer).
[*]Extract this zip into the game folder: Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files. The file ends up as [font=Courier New]mods\CombatGoals\CombatGoals.dll[/font].
[*]Start the game.
[/list]
[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete the folder [font=Courier New]mods\CombatGoals[/font]. Nothing in your save is changed.

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
Tested on game version 0.11.6-alpha.1 with Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3. The game is in early access: an update can break the mod until it is rebuilt.
It uses the game's own textures, fonts and colours, so it looks like part of the game.

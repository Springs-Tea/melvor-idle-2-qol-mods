SUMMARY (the short line under the name, at most 350 characters)
A small window with the XP per hour of every combat skill you are training, a level goal of your own for each, and the time left to it. Like the footer's "time left to level N", for all the skills combat trains at once. While skilling, the end of the footer says when the stop you set ends the action.

DESCRIPTION (paste as is: it is in Nexus formatting)

[size=5][b]Combat Goals[/b][/size]
The game's footer tells you how long a skill needs to reach a level you type in, but only for the one skill you are doing. Combat trains several at once. This is that footer line for every combat skill that is gaining XP.

[size=4][b]Using it[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Start a fight: the window appears by itself, and a round button for it appears in the strip on the right edge of the screen. That button, or [b]F10[/b], closes the window and opens it again; the red X on the window closes it too.
[*]Each line is a skill: your level, its XP per hour, and the time left to the level in the box at the end.
[*]Type your own [b]goal[/b] into that box. It is remembered per character; when you reach it, it moves on to the next level.
[*]The box with the clock at the top right picks the period, like the footer's own: the rate is averaged over the last 1, 5, 10 or 30 minutes.
[*]Drag the window anywhere; drag its bottom right corner for another size. The button beside the X folds it down to its title, to put it aside without closing it; the same button opens it again.
[/list]

[size=4][b]While skilling[/b][/size]
The footer already shows your XP per hour, the time to a level you type in and when the materials run out. What it does not say is when the stop you set will end the action. Smithing, Crafting, Fletching, Runecrafting, Beastcrafting, Cooking and Herblore have stops: set one and start making something, and about ten seconds later the end of the footer says how long until the stop ends the action, worded as the footer words its own times: "[b]1m 38s[/b] until Stop Condition", at the pace of the time period chosen in the footer. Its tooltip names your stop as the skill's stop panel does. A new stop, another recipe, or the game paused or closed starts the measurement again. It never pushes the footer's own parts aside, and it moves along with them as they grow and shrink: in a narrow game window, with no room left at the end of the footer, it is left out. The window is for combat only.

[size=4][b]What the rate is[/b][/size]
The rate of the fight you are in. Leaving combat, or fighting something else, starts the measurement again, so time spent on other skills never drags the rate down. Only skills that are gaining XP right now are listed.

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]The first ten seconds of a fight show "Waiting for combat XP...": a rate needs a little history.
[*]XP gained while the game was closed is not part of the rate.
[*]Outside combat the window hides by itself.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/melvoridle2/mods/2]Melvor Mod Loader[/url] (0.1.3 or newer).
[*]Extract this zip into the game folder: Steam > right-click Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files. The file ends up as [font=Courier New]mods\CombatGoals\CombatGoals.dll[/font].
[*]Start the game.
[/list]
To update, extract the new zip over the old one.

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete the folder [font=Courier New]mods\CombatGoals[/font]. Your goals and the window's place are in settings.json there. Nothing in your save is changed.

Made for game version 0.11.6-alpha.1 with Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3. The game is in early access: an update can break the mod until it is rebuilt. It then switches itself off and says so in MelvorModLoader.log; the game and your other mods keep running.

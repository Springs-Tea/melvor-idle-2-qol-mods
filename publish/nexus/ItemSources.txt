SUMMARY (the short line under the name, at most 350 characters)
Hover "Found in" or "Used in" on an item: which enemy drops it and where, how likely, and every recipe, chest, thieving target or shop purchase that gives or uses it. Press Alt to keep any item tooltip open; its Make button says what it takes to make a number of it. On the game's drop lists: what each chance means in kills.

DESCRIPTION (paste as is: it is in Nexus formatting)

[size=5][b]Item Sources[/b][/size]
The game's item panel says "Found in" and "Used in" and shows a few skill icons. This tells you what is behind them: which enemy, in which area, how many and how likely; which recipe, chest, thieving target or shop purchase.

[size=4][b]In the bank[/b][/size]
Select an item and put the pointer on its "Found in" or "Used in" row. The list opens beside the panel. Nothing to click, nothing to set up.

[size=4][b]Anywhere else: Alt[/b][/size]
The game shows the same small tooltip for an item wherever the pointer is on one: a recipe's materials, a shop, loot. It follows the pointer, so its two rows cannot be reached.
[list=1]
[*]Point at the item, so its tooltip is up.
[*]Press and let go of [b]Alt[/b]: a copy of the tooltip stays where it is.
[*]Move onto its "Found in" or "Used in" row.
[*]Alt again, Esc, or a click somewhere else closes it.
[/list]
So while you look at a recipe: point at the material you are short of, press Alt, and see where it comes from.

[size=4][b]Planning a quantity[/b][/size]
What do I need for a hundred of these? Keep the tooltip of a bar, an arrow, a potion or anything else a skill makes from materials open with Alt, and click [b]Make[/b]. Enter how many you want in the bank and press Enter; the box starts at 100. The list goes from the first material down to the item: how many are needed, how many are in the bank, how many are still to make or get, and the time at your current rate. A row your bank already covers is greyed. Make again folds the list away.
[list]
[*]Chances to save materials, double items or skip steps are not counted, so it may take less.
[*]Requirements you do not meet yet are under their step, the way the game shows them on its recipes.
[*]With several recipes, the plan uses the one you have selected in its skill, and says so.
[*]A crop's time is for one plot, one harvest after another.
[*]Gathering and drop times come from the rate the bottom bar shows; "No rate" means it has none for that item right now.
[*]A material no recipe makes lists where it comes from. Make only reads: it never starts, selects or buys anything.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Reading the list[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Combat[/b] comes first: the enemy, the area it lives in, how many it drops and the chance per kill.
[*][b]A skill's name[/b]: the thieving target, hunting animal or recipe that gives or uses the item.
[*][b]Opening, Shop, Quests[/b]: chests, purchases, rewards.
[*]"1-5" or "x2" is how many at a time; "6.41%" or "always" is the chance each time.
[*]Move from "Found in" onto its list and rest the pointer on a chance: "Half the time within 9 kills. 9 times in 10 within 30."
[*]A long group shows its first eight lines and says how many more there are.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Enemy and thieving drops[/b][/size]
The game lists an enemy's drops in [b]View Enemy Stats[/b], under [b]Drops[/b], and a thieving target's under [b]View Possible Rewards[/b], each with its chance. Item Sources adds on each chance the tooltip of "Found in", and beside a thieving target's name a tag with your successful tries, from the game's own statistics: "Half the time within 9 kills. 9 times in 10 within 30."
[list]
[*]It counts with the chance per kill or per try. Where the window gives a chance inside its table, the tooltip first says the one it counts with: "7.5% per kill."
[*]Thieving counts successful tries, with your bonuses to the common and rare loot chance. A reward under [b]On Completion[/b] counts completions of the heist.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]The chances under "Found in" are the base ones from the game's own drop tables; bonuses to drop chance are not applied. The drop windows count with the chances the window shows.
[*]The list is read from the game's data when a character is opened, so items and enemies a game update adds show up by themselves.
[*]Alt together with another key (Alt+Tab) is not this key.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Install[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install the [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/melvoridle2/mods/2]Melvor Mod Loader[/url] (0.1.3 or newer).
[*]Extract this zip into the game folder: Steam > right-click Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files. The file ends up as [font=Courier New]mods\ItemSources\ItemSources.dll[/font].
[*]Start the game.
[/list]
To update, extract the new zip over the old one.

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete the folder [font=Courier New]mods\ItemSources[/font]. Nothing in your save is changed.

[size=4][b]About[/b][/size]
Made for game version 0.11.6-alpha.1 with Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3. The game is in early access: an update can break the mod until it is rebuilt. It then switches itself off and says so in MelvorModLoader.log; the game and your other mods keep running.
It uses the game's own textures, fonts and colours, so it looks like part of the game. It gives no items, XP or bonuses.

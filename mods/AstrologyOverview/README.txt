ASTROLOGY OVERVIEW {version}
for Melvor Idle 2, requires Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3 or newer

Which constellation is closest to its next unlock? Open Astrology and press the round button with the
star in the strip on the right edge of the screen. It is there only on the Astrology page, and it is lit
while the overview is open.

Constellations with a node choice ready come first, with their names in gold. Then come the others, the
shortest study time first; when no choice is ready, the shortest time is in gold. Under each name: the
knowledge of the next unlock as the constellation screen shows it, the minor nodes the next major node
needs and the bonus every minor node gives. Locked constellations show only what they still miss.
Finished constellations come last.

The time counts whole studies at your current knowledge per study and interval. For the constellation
you are studying, it counts the time left on the current study. Random events and time spent meeting
other requirements are left out.

Click a row to look at that constellation, the same as clicking its card in the game's list. The red X
or the round button closes the overview. Drag its bottom right corner to make it larger or smaller; it
stays above the game's bottom bar. The mod cannot change your study or spend a choice. It has no
settings and writes no files.

INSTALL
  Install Melvor Mod Loader (0.1.3 or newer).
  Extract the zip into the game folder (Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files).
  The file should end up at mods\AstrologyOverview\AstrologyOverview.dll.
  Start the game. Look for "Mod loaded: AstrologyOverview" in MelvorModLoader.log.
  To update, extract the new zip over the old one.

UNINSTALL
  Delete the folder mods\AstrologyOverview. Your save is never changed.

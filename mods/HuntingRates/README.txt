HUNTING RATES {version}
for Melvor Idle 2 (made for 0.11.6-alpha.1), needs Melvor Mod Loader

Where do I hunt for the most XP, catches or gold per hour? On the Hunting page, a round
button on the right edge of the screen opens a window with one line per animal you can see:
its picture, its location, and per hour:

  - Time: how long one catch takes, surveying, tracking and trapping together.
  - Catches: animals caught.
  - XP: Hunting XP.
  - Gold: what the output and the finds sell for.
  - Traps: traps used.

For now these columns show "-", and a note under the list says why: the window does not
know yet how surveying, tracking and trapping join into a catch, when the XP is added, when
the output comes, or when traps and bait are used up. No number of the game's says how the
steps of a hunt add up, and the window does not guess.

Hover a line for the game's numbers of a hunt of that animal, grouped as the Hunting page's
panel groups them: the time of a survey, the steps of tracking with the time and chance of
one, the time and chance of a try of the trap, the chances to skip a step and to try the
trap again, the XP as the page writes it, the tool, the output with the chance of each item,
the rare species with their extra products, what a survey can find, the trap and the bait.
A rare species you have never trapped and a collectable you have never found are not named,
as the journal does. An animal you have not discovered yet gives nothing away: only its name
as the page writes it, its location and what it needs.

The best number of each column is gold, once it has numbers; click a column's name to sort
by it, and again to turn the order around. An animal you cannot hunt yet is greyed, with the
level it needs. The animal the Hunting page shows has a lit outline. With the game's spider
setting on, no spider is shown.

Click a line to open that animal on the Hunting page. While you are not hunting, its
location and the animal are selected through the page's own buttons, as your own clicks
there would. While you hunt, a click selects nothing, since that might change the hunt; it
only shows the hunt instead of the journal, and the animal's card when the page shows its
location. Nothing is started or stopped, and the window stays open where it is.

Until you move it, the window opens at a corner of the Hunting page where it leaves the
page's panels and its animals in sight. Drag the window to move it, drag its bottom right
corner to resize it, fold it down to its title with the button beside the X. On another
page it steps aside and comes back on the Hunting page. Its place, size, fold and sort
order are kept in settings.json in the mod's folder.

INSTALL
  Extract this zip into the game folder (Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files),
  so that the file ends up as  mods\HuntingRates\HuntingRates.dll

UNINSTALL
  Delete the folder mods\HuntingRates

Nothing in your save is changed.

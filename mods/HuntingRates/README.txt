HUNTING RATES {version}
for Melvor Idle 2 (made for 0.11.6-alpha.1), needs Melvor Mod Loader

Where do I hunt for the most XP, catches or gold per hour? On the Hunting page, a round
button on the right edge of the screen opens a window with one line per animal you can see:
its picture, its location, and:

  - Time: how long one catch takes, surveying, tracking and trapping, escapes included.
  - Catches: animals caught per hour.
  - XP: Hunting XP per hour.
  - Gold: what the output of an hour sells for, rare extras too but no collectable.
  - Traps: traps taken from the bank per hour, one every try, preserved or not.

How the steps of a hunt join into a catch is in no number of the game's: it was measured on
a character's own hunting, on Yellowtail Bird and Wild Chicken. An escape sends you back to
tracking with the animal's fewest steps more to go. A line is "-" until the Hunting page has
written how many surveys a hunt of that animal takes: select the animal there once. A
catch's XP and output follow its size, as the Hunting journal records it: the window counts
by the sizes it recorded for your last catches at the animal's level, or at animal level 1
to 7 by the middle of the range the card writes; above that, XP and Gold are "-" until 5
sizes are recorded. The journal forgets them when the game starts; the window keeps them.
The window reads what the page writes whenever the page is on the screen, window open or
not, and keeps it for each of your characters in readings.json, so a line keeps its numbers
from one game to the next; the tooltip says "as the Hunting page last wrote it" for a number
kept from an earlier game.

Hover a line for the whole hour of that animal: the surveys of a hunt, the steps of tracking
and the time of one, the time and chance of a try of the trap, the chance to try again, what
an escape costs, the time of a catch, the Hunting XP and the journal XP, the output with the
chance of each item, the rare species in one line, the trap and the bait with how long the
bank's last, the gold, and what is not counted: the chance to skip a step, the preservation
chance, what surveying finds, and what the animal's level adds to the average (the middle
of XP and output was measured on 6 catches of Yellowtail Bird, level 1 to 7). A rare
species you have never trapped and a collectable you have never found are not named, as the
journal does. An animal you have not discovered yet gives nothing away: only its name as the
page writes it, its location and what it needs. Each picture is drawn as the animal's card on
the Hunting page last drew it, a black shape where the card draws one; until the page has
drawn its picture, a black shape for an animal the journal counts no catch of, in colour
once it counts one.

The best number of each column is gold; click a column's name to sort by it, and again to
turn the order around. An animal you cannot hunt yet is greyed, with the level it needs.
The animal the Hunting page shows has a lit outline. With the game's spider setting on, no
spider is shown.

Click a line to open that animal on the Hunting page. While you are not hunting, its
location and the animal are selected through the page's own buttons, as your own clicks
there would. While you hunt, a click selects nothing, since that might change the hunt; it
only shows the hunt instead of the journal, and the animal's card when the page shows its
location. Nothing is started or stopped, and the window stays open where it is.

Until you move it, the window opens at the corner of the Hunting page where the most of
its animals and locations stay in sight beside it. Drag the window to move it, drag its
bottom right corner to resize it, fold it down to its title with the button beside the X.
On another page it steps aside and comes back on the Hunting page. Its place, size, fold
and sort order are kept in settings.json in the mod's folder, what the page wrote and the
sizes of your catches in readings.json; deleting that only brings back the "-" until the
page shows the animals again and 5 more catches are recorded.

INSTALL
  Extract this zip into the game folder (Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files),
  so that the file ends up as  mods\HuntingRates\HuntingRates.dll

UNINSTALL
  Delete the folder mods\HuntingRates

Nothing in your save is changed.

AWAY SUMMARY {version}
for Melvor Idle 2, needs Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3 or newer

Switch to another app or minimise the game while your character trains. Come back after 15 minutes
by default to see how long you were away and what changed.

READING THE SUMMARY
  The changes are listed under the same headings as in the game's own summary after a restart:
  skill XP and levels gained, items that entered or left your bank, and the change in gold. An item
  can appear under both gains and uses. Arrows and other items used up from your equipment or a tool
  belt have headings of their own. Mastery XP and other currencies are not counted. Only changes
  appear. Stops and defeats come last: a stop shows the game's reason and how long after you left it
  happened, and a defeat names the item and quantity you lost when the game reports a loss.

OPENING IT AGAIN
  The round Away Summary button on the right opens the last summary and its settings. Change Minutes
  away to a whole number from 1 to 1,440 (one day), or clear Away summaries to stop collecting and
  showing new summaries. Drag the window to move it, drag its bottom right corner to resize it, or
  fold it down beside the X. Its position, size and fold are kept in mods\AwaySummary\settings.json.

GOOD TO KNOW
  A shorter absence keeps your last summary. Changing characters or restarting clears it. When the
  game itself catches up, after a restart or after the computer slept, it shows its own Welcome Back!
  window. Away Summary skips that absence. If a game update breaks the mod, look for
  [AwaySummary] Stopped in MelvorModLoader.log.

INSTALL
  Extract this zip into the game folder (Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files),
  so that the file ends up as  mods\AwaySummary\AwaySummary.dll

UNINSTALL
  Delete the folder mods\AwaySummary.

Nothing in your save is changed.

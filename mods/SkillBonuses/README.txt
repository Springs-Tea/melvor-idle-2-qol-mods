SKILL BONUSES {version}
for Melvor Idle 2 (made for 0.11.6-alpha.1), needs Melvor Mod Loader

Where a skill's bonuses come from. On a skill page with a stats panel, the round button on
the right edge of the screen opens a window with every stat of that panel (the chance to
double, to keep materials, the XP, the action time...), its total as the page writes it,
and under it every source the game counts for it, the largest first: an item, a pet, a
shop purchase, a constellation. Where a page has many trees, rocks, targets or plots, a
drop-down picks which one. "Other" under a total means the listed sources do not add up
to it. The window only reads: nothing is bought, worn, selected or started from it.

Drag the window to move it, drag its bottom right corner to resize it, fold it down to
its title with the button beside the X. Its place, size and fold are kept in
settings.json in the mod's folder.

INSTALL
  Extract this zip into the game folder (Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files),
  so that the file ends up as  mods\SkillBonuses\SkillBonuses.dll

UNINSTALL
  Delete the folder mods\SkillBonuses

Nothing in your save is changed.

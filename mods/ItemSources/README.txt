ITEM SOURCES {version}
for Melvor Idle 2 (tested on 0.11.6-alpha.1), needs Melvor Mod Loader

The game's item panel says "Found in" and "Used in" with a few skill icons. Hover either row and
this lists what is behind them:

  Found in   which enemy drops the item and where that enemy lives, which thieving target,
             hunting animal, chest, recipe, quest or shop purchase gives it, how many, and how
             likely.
  Used in    every recipe, upgrade, shop purchase or animal that uses it, and how many.

Anywhere in the game: press Alt while an item's tooltip is up (on a recipe's materials, in a shop,
on loot) and the tooltip stays open, so you can move onto its two rows. Alt again, Esc or a click
somewhere else closes it.

What it takes to make a number of something: on a tooltip kept open with Alt, of a bar, an arrow,
a potion or anything else a skill makes from materials, click Make. Enter how many you want in
the bank and press Enter; the box starts at 100. The list goes from the first material down to
the item: how many are needed, in the bank, still to make or get, and the time at your current
rate. A row your bank already covers is greyed. Click Make again to fold the list away. It only
reads: nothing is started, selected or bought.

Chances to save materials, double items or skip steps are not counted, so it may take less.
With several recipes, the plan uses the one selected in its skill. A crop's time is for one
plot, one harvest after another. Gathering and drop times use the rate the bottom bar shows;
"No rate" means it has none for that item right now. Requirements you do not meet yet are shown
under their step, and a material no recipe makes lists where it comes from.

What a chance means in kills: move the pointer from "Found in" onto its list (it stays open on
the way) and rest it on a chance. A small tooltip says, for example, "Half the time within 9 kills.
9 times in 10 within 30." Kills for an enemy, tries for a pick, a chest or a catch. This works in
the bank and on a tooltip kept open with Alt.

The chances are worked out from the game's own drop tables. The list is read from the game's
data when a character is opened, so items and enemies a game update adds show up by themselves.

INSTALL
  Extract this zip into the game folder (Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files),
  so that the file ends up as  mods\ItemSources\ItemSources.dll

UNINSTALL
  Delete the folder mods\ItemSources

Nothing in your save is changed.

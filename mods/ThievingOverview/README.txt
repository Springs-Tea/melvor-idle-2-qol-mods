THIEVING OVERVIEW {version}
for Melvor Idle 2 (made for 0.11.6-alpha.1), needs Melvor Mod Loader 0.1.3 or newer

Which target should I steal from, for XP, for gold or for one item, and will it knock me out?
On the Thieving page, a round button on the right edge of the screen opens a window with one
line per target, of the place the Thieving page shows or of every place you have found, and
per hour of stealing there:

  - Success: the game's own chance that one try succeeds.
  - Time: one try, with the stun after a failure on average.
  - XP: Thieving XP.
  - Gold: coins plus what the items sell for.
  - Rare: how long until a rare item, on average.
  - Food: about how much of the food chosen for Thieving you eat.
  - Safe or Risky: whether one failure can take you from your auto eat point to 0 HP.

The best number of each column is gold; click a column's name to sort by it, and again to
turn the order around. The target you are stealing from has a lit outline. A target you
cannot steal from yet is greyed, with what it still needs; one the game's Thieving cards show
as ??? is ??? here too, without its picture. Hover a line for the whole hour: every item and
what it sells for, the coins, the damage, the food and how long yours lasts, your record.

Best for: choose an item and see the targets that give it, the most per hour first.

On the game's own screens: in View Possible Rewards, rest the pointer on the quantity of a
reward, or of the coins, for how many of it that target gives per hour. While you steal from
a Risky target, its card has a small Risky tag after its name.

The numbers come from the game's own values for each target, so your stealth, bonuses and
distractions are in them. Chances count as their long-run average. A heist's XP and rewards
are not counted: for a heist only success, time, food and Safe are shown. With a fortune, a
target's items are counted once you have opened its View Possible Rewards. Food counts every
failure that hurts at the target's max hit: a guide, not an exact count. It is an estimate,
not a promise.

Click a line to go to that target's place: the Thieving page opens it, as its own Select
Location does. Nothing is started or stopped, no target is chosen, and the window stays open.

Drag the window to move it, drag its bottom right corner to resize it, fold it down to its
title with the button beside the X. On another page it steps aside and comes back on the
Thieving page. Its place, size, fold, choices and sort orders are kept in settings.json in
the mod's folder.

INSTALL
  Extract this zip into the game folder (Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files),
  so that the file ends up as  mods\ThievingOverview\ThievingOverview.dll

UNINSTALL
  Delete the folder mods\ThievingOverview

Nothing in your save is changed.

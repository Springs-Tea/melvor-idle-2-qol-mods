MINING RATES {version}
for Melvor Idle 2 (made for 0.11.6-alpha.1), needs Melvor Mod Loader

Where do I mine for the most XP, ore or gold per hour? On the Mining page, a round button on
the right edge of the screen opens a window with one line per rock you can see: the rock's
picture, its category, and:

  - Time: how long one ore takes, with the hits a break takes counted in.
  - Ore: the rock's ore per hour.
  - XP: Mining XP per hour.
  - Gold: what the ore and the other items of an hour sell for.

The best number of each column is gold; click a column's name to sort by it, and again to
turn the order around. A rock you cannot mine yet is greyed, with the level it needs. The
rock you are mining has a lit outline. A rock you hid on the Mining page is listed only
while the page lists hidden rocks too. Hover a line for the whole hour: the time of a hit,
the breaks, the XP per hour and per hit, the ore of a break and of an hour, every item of a
hit with its chance and how many an hour brings, and the gold.

The numbers come from the game's own values for each rock, so whatever changes those changes
the window too. The ore comes when the rock breaks. How many hits that takes follows the
game's own words on a rock's break chance: "Your chance to break the rock per hit. Rock
automatically breaks when a sum of 100% is reached." The window reads them as: every hit
breaks the rock at its break chance, and the hit that brings the sum of those chances to
100% breaks it for sure. Three more things are the window's reading too: every hit gives the
rock's XP, whether it breaks the rock or not; the doubling chance doubles the ore and the
items of a hit; and a minimum break bar is where the sum starts after a break. Chances count
as their long-run average. It is an estimate, not a promise. A chance to shatter a rock, a
chance of more ore, and a rock's own stone, essence or bar are not counted; the tooltip says
so when you have one. Mining has no mastery XP in this game, so there is no column for it.

Click a line to see that rock on the Mining page: the page's category filter goes to the
rock's category when it is on another one, and its list of rocks scrolls to the rock's card.
Nothing is started or stopped, and the window stays open. A rock the game has no picture
for yet shows none.

Drag the window to move it, drag its bottom right corner to resize it, fold it down to its
title with the button beside the X. On another page it steps aside and comes back on the
Mining page. Its place, size, fold and sort order are kept in settings.json in the mod's
folder.

INSTALL
  Extract this zip into the game folder (Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files),
  so that the file ends up as  mods\MiningRates\MiningRates.dll

UNINSTALL
  Delete the folder mods\MiningRates

Nothing in your save is changed.

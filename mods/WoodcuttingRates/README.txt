WOODCUTTING RATES {version}
for Melvor Idle 2 (made for 0.11.6-alpha.1), needs Melvor Mod Loader

Which tree gives me the most XP per hour? On the Woodcutting page, a round button on the
right edge of the screen opens a window with one line per tree you can see: its picture,
its category, and:

  - Time: how long one log takes.
  - Logs: the tree's logs per hour.
  - XP: Woodcutting XP per hour.
  - Gold: what the logs and the other items of an hour sell for.

The hour rests on the cuts of an hour: the logs come with a cut, as a tree's card lists
them under "Full Cut", and so does the XP. The window works out the hits a cut takes from
the tree's hit points and your chances.

The best number of each column is gold; click a column's name to sort by it, and again to
turn the order around. A tree you cannot cut yet is greyed, with the level it needs. The
tree the Woodcutting page shows has a lit outline. Hover a line for the hour at that tree:
the time of a hit, the XP per hour and per cut, the tree's hit points, the hits a cut takes
and the cuts of an hour, your chances of double damage, a weak spot and an instant cut, the
logs, every item a hit or a cut can bring with its chance, and the overgrowth on the tree.

The numbers come from the game's own values for each tree, so whatever changes those
changes the window too. Measured in the game, on 597 hits of a Maple Tree: a hit takes 1
hit point and a double hit 2, and the XP comes with a cut only (on 88 of 88 cuts, on none
of 509 other hits), the XP tag's "The Skill XP gained per action." with a cut as the
action. A weak spot and an instant cut did not come there, so they follow the game's own
words: a weak spot takes "3 damage + 10% of the Tree's Max HP", read as it says, not
rounded; an instant cut takes the tree's hit points to 0. The items of a hit come on every
hit and those of a cut on every cut, as the card lists them under "Per Hit" and "Full
Cut". Chances count as their long-run average. It is an estimate, not a promise. A tree
you hid on the Woodcutting page is listed only while the page lists hidden trees too.

Click a line to see that tree on the Woodcutting page: the page shows its category if its
card is hidden, and its list scrolls until the whole card is in sight beside the window.
When the page has no room for the card there, the line's tooltip says so: drag the window
aside. Nothing is started or stopped, and the window stays open where it is.

Until you move it, the window opens at the right side of the page, beside the trees' cards.
Drag the window to move it, drag its bottom right corner to resize it, fold it down to its
title with the button beside the X. On another page it steps aside and comes back on the
Woodcutting page. Its place, size, fold and sort order are kept in settings.json in the
mod's folder.

INSTALL
  Extract this zip into the game folder (Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files),
  so that the file ends up as  mods\WoodcuttingRates\WoodcuttingRates.dll

UNINSTALL
  Delete the folder mods\WoodcuttingRates

Nothing in your save is changed.

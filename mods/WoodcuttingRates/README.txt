WOODCUTTING RATES {version}
for Melvor Idle 2 (made for 0.11.6-alpha.1), needs Melvor Mod Loader

Which tree gives me the most XP per hour? On the Woodcutting page, a round button on the
right edge of the screen opens a window with one line per tree you can see: its picture,
its category, and per hour:

  - Time: how long one log takes, every hit of a cut counted in.
  - Logs: logs per hour.
  - XP: Woodcutting XP.
  - Gold: what the logs and items of an hour sell for.

Time, Logs and Gold show - for now: a tree takes several hits to cut, and the game gives
no number for the damage of a hit, so the window does not count the hits a cut takes and
does not guess them.

The best number of each column is gold; click a column's name to sort by it, and again to
turn the order around. A tree you cannot cut yet is greyed, with the level it needs. The
tree being cut has a lit outline. Hover a line for the hour as far as it is counted: the
time of a hit, the XP per hour and per hit, the tree's hit points and your chances of
double damage, a weak spot and an instant cut, the logs, every item a hit or a cut can
bring with its chance, and the overgrowth on the tree now.

The numbers come from the game's own values for each tree, so whatever changes those
changes the window too. Every hit gives the tree's XP, and chances count as their
long-run average. It is an estimate, not a promise. A tree you hid on the Woodcutting
page is listed only while the page's "Show hidden" is ticked.

Click a line to show that tree on the Woodcutting page: the page shows its category if
its card is hidden, and scrolls until the card is in sight. Nothing is started or
stopped, and the window stays open.

Drag the window to move it, drag its bottom right corner to resize it, fold it down to its
title with the button beside the X. On another page it steps aside and comes back on the
Woodcutting page. Its place, size, fold and sort order are kept in settings.json in the
mod's folder.

INSTALL
  Extract this zip into the game folder (Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files),
  so that the file ends up as  mods\WoodcuttingRates\WoodcuttingRates.dll

UNINSTALL
  Delete the folder mods\WoodcuttingRates

Nothing in your save is changed.

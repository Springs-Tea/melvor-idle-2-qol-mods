ITEM ENCYCLOPEDIA {version}
for Melvor Idle 2 (tested on 0.11.6-alpha.1), needs Melvor Mod Loader

Every item of the game in one window. F9, or the round button with the book in the strip of
round buttons on the right edge of the screen, opens it. (The game hides that strip in the bank
and the shop; F9 works everywhere.)

  Search     by name or description.
  Sort       by the game's order, name, level, sell price, or how many you own.
  Filters    by kind (weapon, armour, food, potion, tool, material...), slot, combat style
             (melee, ranged, magic), weapon (bow, sword, staff...), skill (what it gives, what it
             uses, its tools) and where an item comes from: an enemy drop, a skill, the shop, a
             chest, thieving, a quest, an upgrade, or one area or dungeon. Tick several to combine
             them: Ranged and Torso, or Cooking, or everything that drops in one dungeon.
  Show       only what you own, only what you have found, or only what you have not found yet.
             "No spoilers" keeps what you have not found as ???.
  Recent     your last five different searches and filter choices, on top of the filters: one
             click and the list is back to it.
  Details    the game's own item panel with every stat, bonus and requirement; how many you
             have; every enemy that drops it with its area, amount and chance; every recipe that
             makes it with its level and ingredients; chests, thieving targets, shop purchases,
             quests; and everything it is used in. Click an ingredient to open it, Back to return.
             Rest the pointer on a chance under "Found in" and a small tooltip says what it
             means in kills or tries: "Half the time within 9 kills. 9 times in 10 within 30."
             View Item Stats, under anything worn in combat, opens the game's own item stats
             window for it, also for gear you do not have yet: every bonus compared with what
             you wear, TAB for the other loadouts. Nothing is equipped. While that window is
             open the encyclopedia steps aside; it is back when that window closes. F9 closes
             the window for you and brings the encyclopedia back.

Drag the window from any empty spot, and its bottom right corner to resize it. The small button
beside the X folds it down to its title, to put it aside without closing it; the same button or
F9 opens it again. Its place, size and filters are remembered in settings.json in the mod's folder.

Everything is read from the game's data, so items a game update adds show up by themselves. The
mod only shows: it never gives, buys or equips anything.

INSTALL
  Extract this zip into the game folder (Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files),
  so that the file ends up as  mods\ItemEncyclopedia\ItemEncyclopedia.dll

UNINSTALL
  Delete the folder mods\ItemEncyclopedia

Nothing in your save is changed.

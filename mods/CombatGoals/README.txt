COMBAT GOALS {version}
for Melvor Idle 2 (tested on 0.11.6-alpha.1), needs Melvor Mod Loader

The game's footer tells you how long a skill takes to reach a level goal, but only for one skill.
Combat trains several at once. This adds a small window that measures the XP per hour of every
combat skill you are training and shows the time left to a level goal you set for each of them.

  - Only skills that are gaining XP are listed.
  - Type a goal level per skill. Goals are remembered per character.
  - The rate is the average of the current fight over the last 1, 5, 10 or 30 minutes. Leaving
    combat, or fighting something else, starts the measurement again.
  - Drag the window anywhere. Drag its bottom right corner for another size.
  - A round button on the right edge of the screen, and F10, show and hide it.

WHILE SKILLING
  Smithing, Crafting, Fletching, Runecrafting, Beastcrafting, Cooking and Herblore have stops.
  Set one and start making something: about ten seconds later the end of the footer says how
  long until the stop ends the action, worded as the footer words its own times, for example
  "1m 38s until Stop Condition", at the pace of the time period chosen in the footer.
  Its tooltip names your stop as the skill's stop panel does.
  When the footer has no room left at its end (a narrow game window), it is left out.
  The window is for combat only.

INSTALL
  Extract this zip into the game folder (Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files),
  so that the file ends up as  mods\CombatGoals\CombatGoals.dll

UNINSTALL
  Delete the folder mods\CombatGoals

Nothing in your save is changed. Settings are kept in mods\CombatGoals\settings.json

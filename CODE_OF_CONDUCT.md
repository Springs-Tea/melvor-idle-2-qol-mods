# Code of conduct

This is a small fan project about a game people enjoy. Keep it that way.

## What is expected

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are playing a game, not doing their job.
- Talk about the code, the mod or the idea, not about the person.
- Assume a report or a question is made in good faith, and answer it that way.
- Say so when you do not know or could not try something. It helps more than a confident guess.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or attacks on who someone is.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Pressure on maintainers or contributors: nobody owes anyone a feature or a deadline.
- Asking for, or sharing, the game's files, paid content, or ways around buying the game.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to the repository owner through GitHub (the "Report content" link on the comment, or a private
message to [@Springs-Tea](https://github.com/Springs-Tea)). Reports are read and kept confidential. Comments
that break these rules may be edited or removed, and people who keep breaking them may be blocked from the
repository.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place this project is discussed under its
name.
